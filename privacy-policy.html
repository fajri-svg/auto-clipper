🎬 OrcaClip — Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: May 9, 2026

1. About OrcaClip

OrcaClip is an automated video clipping tool that processes publicly available YouTube videos into short-form vertical clips with auto-generated subtitles. Content is only clipped from videos where the original creator has given permission.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Data We Collect

OrcaClip does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users or viewers. The tool operates locally on the user's own machine.

2.2 Third-Party Services

OrcaClip uses the following third-party services:

  • YouTube / yt-dlp — to download source videos (public content only)
  • Facebook Graph API — to publish clips to the OrcaClip Facebook Page
  • TikTok API — to publish clips to the OrcaClip TikTok account

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share any user data with them beyond what is required for content publishing.

2.3 Cookies

OrcaClip does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

2.4 Data Retention

No personal data is retained. Video clips are stored locally and are not shared with third parties beyond the publishing platforms listed above.

3. User Data Deletion

OrcaClip does not store any user data. If you would like to request deletion of any content posted by OrcaClip, please contact us at the email below and we will remove it within 7 business days.
