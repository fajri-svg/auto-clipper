Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how OrcaClip ("we", "our", or "the application") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our content automation platform.

1. About OrcaClip

OrcaClip is a content creation platform that helps creators automatically download YouTube videos, generate short-form clips with AI-powered viral scoring, add subtitles, and schedule them to social media platforms including Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

2. Information We Collect

OrcaClip collects and processes the following types of information:

  • Social media access tokens — Facebook Page tokens and Instagram access tokens provided by the user through Meta's Graph API, stored locally on the user's device in orca_config.json.
  • TikTok access tokens — OAuth 2.0 access tokens obtained through TikTok's official login flow, stored locally on the user's device.
  • YouTube video URLs — URLs entered by the user to process and clip content.
  • Video content — Downloaded video files processed locally on the user's device.
  • Caption and hashtag data — Text generated or edited by the user for social media posts.

3. How We Use Your Information

OrcaClip uses the collected information exclusively to:

  • Download and process YouTube videos as directed by the user
  • Generate short-form video clips with subtitles and hook text
  • Upload and schedule video content to Facebook Pages via the Meta Graph API
  • Upload and schedule video content to Instagram accounts via the Meta Graph API
  • Upload and schedule video content to TikTok accounts via the TikTok Content Posting API
  • Back up processed clips to the user's personal MEGA cloud storage
  • Log clip activity to Google Sheets as configured by the user

4. Data Storage

OrcaClip operates primarily as a local application. All processing occurs on the user's own device. We do not operate central servers that store your personal data, videos, or social media tokens.

  • Access tokens are stored locally in orca_config.json on the user's Windows machine
  • Processed video clips are stored locally in the user's designated output directory
  • No data is transmitted to OrcaClip's servers beyond what is necessary to operate the application interface

5. Third-Party Services

OrcaClip integrates with the following third-party services. Their respective privacy policies govern how they handle your data:

  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • TikTok — TikTok Privacy Policy
  • YouTube / Google — Google Privacy Policy
  • MEGA — MEGA Privacy Policy

6. TikTok Data Usage

When using OrcaClip with TikTok, we access the following TikTok API scopes on your behalf:

  • video.upload — To upload video content to your TikTok account
  • video.publish — To publish or schedule uploaded videos

OrcaClip does not access, store, or share your TikTok profile data, followers, comments, messages, or any data beyond what is necessary to post and schedule videos on your behalf. Your TikTok access token is stored only on your local device and is never transmitted to OrcaClip's servers.

7. Data Sharing

OrcaClip does not sell, trade, or share your personal data, access tokens, or content with any third parties, except as required to operate the social media posting features described above (i.e., transmitting your content to the respective platform APIs as directed by you).

8. Data Retention

OrcaClip does not retain user data on any central server. Local files (clips, tokens, logs) remain on the user's device and can be deleted at any time. Access tokens can be revoked at any time through the respective platform's settings.

9. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke OrcaClip's access to your Facebook/Instagram account via Facebook App Settings
  • Revoke OrcaClip's access to your TikTok account via TikTok's Connected Apps settings
  • Delete all local data by removing the OrcaClip application files from your device

10. Children's Privacy

OrcaClip is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
